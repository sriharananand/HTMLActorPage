HIP HOP TAMIZHA

Hip Hop Tamizha is an Indian musical duo consisting of Adhi and Jeeva. They are known for their work in the Tamil film industry, particularly for their contributions to Tamil hip hop music. The duo has gained popularity for their unique style, blending traditional Tamil music with contemporary hip hop elements. They have released several hit songs and albums, and have also composed music for various Tamil films. Hip Hop Tamizha has a significant fan following and continues to influence the music scene in India.
